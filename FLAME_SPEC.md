# FLAME first experience

## Product

FLAME is an evidence-led exploration of NASA microgravity combustion research for the Space Apps challenge “Flame in Freefall.” Explore gives a concise visual explanation; Research exposes conditions, findings, sources and limits. Both modes use the same selected experiment and keep that selection when switching modes.

## Scientific records

- **FLEX / FLEX-145:** NASA describes FLEX as an ISS investigation of liquid-droplet flammability, extinction and gaseous suppression. Conditions and reported values in the interface are attributed specifically to FLEX-145: methanol, initial droplet diameter 3.330 mm, pressure 763.468 mmHg, 19.9% O₂ / 74.9% N₂ / 5.0% CO₂, extinction diameter 1.613 mm and average burning-rate constant 0.448 mm²/s. These are not general limits. Sources: [NASA PSI-69](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-69), [NASA NTRS FLEX-145 paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20130010979/downloads/20130010979.pdf), [NASA SVS 11318](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/11318/).
- **BASS-II:** NASA describes solid-fuel tests in a contained flow duct, varying oxygen concentration, ventilation, and fuel type, thickness and geometry. The summary says most heat-release rates were below 100 W and estimates a mean global equivalence ratio of 1.3. These aggregate findings are not assigned to a single test. Source: [NASA NTRS 20210011385](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210011385).

NASA SVS 11318 is the source of the locally bundled FLEX archival video and still. BASS-II’s diagram is a FLAME schematic labeled as illustrative; it is not NASA footage or a measurement. No live telemetry is displayed.

## Rendering and motion

FLEX footage is delivered at NASA SVS’s 960×540 source resolution, and the still is the available 1024×576 NASA asset. The interface does not claim 4K detail or apply artificial sharpness. Footage retains its archival color; the BASS-II SVG is explicitly a concept drawing. There are no rendered 3D experiment objects, so physically based shading, geometric shadows and bloom are not used. The central scene uses a cinematic still/video frame, not a reconstructed flame.

GSAP coordinates the introduction, mode and Evidence Lens camera framing, playback focus and experiment-change focus. Tweens are killed and retargeted from their current transform; reduced-motion preferences remove camera movement. Rendering profiles use browser device-memory, core-count and data-saver hints to reduce backdrop effects on constrained devices. They do not alter the source media or scientific values. These are heuristics, not a GPU benchmark.

## AI and boundaries

`GET /api/ask` returns only whether server-side AI is available, so the interface checks runtime configuration without baking the key state into a static page. `POST /api/ask` accepts a selected experiment and a bounded question, builds context only from the local sourced record, and calls OpenRouter from the server using `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` (optional `OPENROUTER_MODEL`, default `openai/gpt-4o-mini`). The key is never sent to the client. Without the key the interface and route report that AI is unavailable and do not return a fabricated answer. Verified source URLs are attached from the local record, not generated by the model.

This prototype presents published evidence and explanatory context. It is not a validated combustion model, operational fire-safety tool, or substitute for the original report.

### Corpus and verification

`lib/research/corpus.ts` contains nine stable evidence records, validated by strict Zod schemas at load time. Each has an objective, material, platform, conditions with their role, finding, source URL/locator, verification date, availability and explicit missing parameters. There are four sources: NTRS BASS-II 20210011385 (abstract, fetched through NASA's citation API), PSI-69 version 5 (Project/Objectives/Approach/Hardware), SVS 11318 (description and video caption), and NTRS 20130010979 (slide 10, visually inspected from the PDF). The challenge page returned 502; its contents were not verified. PSI's home page is a directory, not an experimental result.

- BASS-SETUP, BASS-HEAT, BASS-GASES: document-level qualitative evidence. “Most well below 100 W” is a bound on calculated heat-release rates, not an exact scalar or direct heat measurement.
- BASS-RATIO: derived aggregate mean equivalence ratio, 1.3 (dimensionless); uncertainty and sample count unavailable in the abstract.
- FLEX-OBJECTIVE: investigation objectives and protocol; the 0.5–1.5 atm range is not a measured outcome.
- FLEX-COOL: qualitative media observation; its run and conditions are not identified.
- FLEX-145-INITIAL and FLEX-145-EXTINCTION: published measurement annotations, 3.330 mm and 1.613 mm. No raw images were reprocessed.
- FLEX-145-RATE: published derived average burning-rate constant, 0.448 mm²/s.

**Source conflict:** slide 9 and slide 10 of the FLEX presentation disagree on diameters and gas proportions. Records explicitly select slide 10's plot inset, disclose the conflict, retain reported fractions without renormalization and do not imply uncertainty has been resolved. Pressure in Pa is a derived conversion using 101325 Pa / 760 mmHg. No BASS trial-level data or raw FLEX time series were ingested.

### Deterministic retrieval and scoring

`lib/research/engine.ts` performs local token matching with explicit synonyms, optional experiment scope and stable ID tie-breaking. No database, vector service or remote crawl is required. Ranking is FLAME search relevance, **not NASA credibility, physical hazard, or probability**:

- Up to 45 points: fraction of question tokens present in record text.
- Up to 20: fraction in title/topic fields, preventing generic shared context from dominating.
- Up to 10: fraction in condition text. This is a lexical condition match, not trial equivalence.
- Directness: measured/derived 8, qualitative finding 6, media 4, description 2.
- Numerical availability: measured 6, derived 3, absent 0.
- Comparability: 5 only when the selected reference passes the comparison engine.
- Source completeness: 6 for URL, precise locator and verification date.

Each result exposes the components. Scores are bounded by 100 but are not probabilities. Up to six records are returned. Empty queries browse records. Coverage is a conservative lexical heuristic, not proof that a question is answerable. Unsupported environments/materials/safety conclusions, missing requested numerical quantities, low overlap and unknown terms produce explicit gaps. “Not found in this corpus” never means “not studied by NASA.” This small English-language evaluation is not a benchmark of general scientific understanding.

### Comparisons

Comparisons validate records, quantities, units, measured/derived status, statistics and experimental context. Units normalize to SI before subtraction; results display in record A's units. Unmatched/missing cross-trial pressure, gas, flow, support, fuel or stage block numerical comparison. Solid versus droplet systems always receive qualitative context and sources.

One real quantitative comparison is available: the initial and extinction diameters of **the same FLEX-145 run**. B minus A is −1.717 mm, labeled derived within-run change, not an independent-trial comparison, rate, hazard percentage or significance test. The source conflict and unavailable uncertainty remain visible. No uncertainty is invented. Comparison test fixtures with changed units are not added to the scientific corpus.

### Grounded AI

The installed AI SDK 7 `generateText` / `Output.object` and OpenRouter `createOpenRouter` APIs were inspected locally. `POST /api/ask` retrieves only the selected experiment's records, supplies fixed evidence IDs, and requests structured claims classified as reported facts or interpretations. Zod restricts IDs to the retrieved set. A second validation rejects foreign IDs, model-authored links/inline citations and numerical claim values absent from cited records. Links and locators come from the corpus. Citation membership and numerical presence do **not** establish semantic entailment; source review is still necessary. Unsupported questions return a labeled deterministic limitation without invoking a model.

`OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is required only on the server; `OPENROUTER_MODEL` optionally overrides `openai/gpt-4o-mini`. Missing configuration returns an honest unavailable state. Requests have bounded input/output, no retries and a 45-second timeout. Keys are never serialized/logged. Deployment secrets remain ignored by Git. No key was configured during verification, so **no live AI provider response was verified**. Distributed rate limiting and an external scientific review remain deployment follow-ups.
